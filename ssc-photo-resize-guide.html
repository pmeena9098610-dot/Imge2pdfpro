SSC Form Photo Rejection — Top 5 Reasons

SSC (Staff Selection Commission) Bharti Prakriya, Exam Pattern aur Form Guidelines ki Vistrit Jankari

SSC (Staff Selection Commission) ke madhyam se sarkaari naukri paana lakho ummeedwaro ka ek bada sapna hota hai. Har saal is ayog dvara vibhin pado ke liye vigyapti jari ki jati hai jisme administrative, technical, aur clerical post shamil hoti hain. Ek achi post par selection aapke career ko naye mukam par le ja sakta hai. Is post me hum SSC (Staff Selection Commission) ki selection process, form bharne ke zaroori niyam, aur photo/signature specification ke baare me sab kuch cover karenge, taaki apko form bharte waqt kisi pareshani ka samna na karna pade.

Selection Process (Chayan Prakriya)

Aam taur par, is exam ke madhyam se selection ke 3 pramukh charan (stages) hote hain. Candidate ko har stage ko qualify karna padta hai:

  • Stage 1 (Preliminary Exam): Ye ek screening test hota hai jisme objective type (MCQ) questions puche jate hain. Isme General Knowledge, Aptitude aur Current Affairs ka test hota hai. Iske marks final merit me nahi judte, ye sirf mains me jane ke liye qualifying hota hai.
  • Stage 2 (Mains Exam): Pre nikalne wale candidates ko Mains exam dena hota hai. Ye generally descriptive nature (likhit) ya tough level ke MCQ ka hota hai. Asli competition yahi par hota hai aur iske marks merit tay পণ্ডিত tay karte hain.
  • Stage 3 (Interview / Skill Test): Jo post interview wali hoti hain, unme personality test hota hai. Clerical post ke liye typing ya shorthand test liya jata hai.

Eligibility aur Qualification

Form bharne ke liye sabse pehle yogyata check karni chahiye. Age limit general category ke liye zyada tar 21 se 40 saal tak hoti hai (reservation rules ke hisaab se relaxation milta hai). Educational qualification post ke hisaab se alag hoti hai. Kuch posts ke liye 12th pass aur computer diploma maanga jata hai, jabki officer grade ke liye kisi bhi stream me graduation anivarya hota hai. Domicile rule ke anusar, state ke bahar ke candidates general me count hote hain.

Photo & Signature Guidelines for SSC (Staff Selection Commission) Application Form

Bahut se candidates mehnat se tayari karte hain lekin online form bharte samay photo aur signature ke chote se galat format ki wajah se unka admit card hold par chala jata hai. Aise me saal bhar ki mehnat bekar ho jati hai. Isliye ayog ke in instructions ko dhyan se follow karein:

  • File Size Constraints: Photo ka aakar (size) generally 20 KB se 50 KB ke beech hona chahiye. Wahin signature ka size 10 KB se 20 KB ke beech nirdharit hota hai.
  • File Format: Sirf .jpg ya .jpeg format hi server dwara accept kiya jata hai. .png ya .pdf file nahi chalegi.
  • Background: Photo ka background hamesha safed (White) ya halke rang (Light color) ka hona anivarya hai. Dark background wali photo reject ho sakti hai.
  • Clarity aur Dimension: Photo passport size honi chahiye jisme aapka chehra (Face) kam se kam 70% se 80% jagah cover kare. Aankhein seedhi camera me dekhti hui honi chahiye. Signature kaali ya neeli syahi (Black/Blue ink) se safed paper par kiya hona chahiye. Signature block letters (capital letters) me na karein.

Preparation Strategy (Tayari Kaise Karein?)

Is pariksha ko paas karne ke liye consistent padhai ki zaroorat hai. Sabse pehle syllabus ki hard copy nikal kar apne study table par rakhein. Previous year papers (PYQ) ko solve karein taaki exam ke trend ka pata chal sake. State level GK ke liye kisi achi standard book ko follow karein aur roj news paper padhkar current affairs ke notes banayein.
