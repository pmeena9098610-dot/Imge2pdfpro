Passport Photo Ghar Par Banayein — Complete DIY Guide

Photo studio mein passport photo ke liye ₹100-300 lagta hai — aur phir bhi kabhi kabhi size ya background galat aata hai aur portal reject karta hai. Ghar par mobile se professional quality passport photo banana actually bahut easy hai agar sahi technique follow karein.

Setup — Ghar Par Photo Studio Kaise Banayein

  1. Background: White bedsheet ya chart paper wall par tape karein — wrinkles ya shadows nahi aane chahiye
  2. Lighting: Window ke saamne khade hon (natural light) — artificial light se uneven shadows aate hain
  3. Camera Position: Phone ko eye-level par rakhein (table/stack of books par) — tilt angle se face distort hota hai
  4. Distance: 4-5 feet door khade hon camera se — zoom use mat karein, digital zoom quality kharab karta hai
  5. Timer: 3-second timer lagayein taaki hastily click karne se blurry photo na aaye
