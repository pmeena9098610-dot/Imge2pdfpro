Indian Passport Photo Rules (2024 Updated)

  • Size: 3.5 x 4.5 cm (2x2 inch bhi acceptable)
  • Background: Plain white or off-white
  • Face Coverage: 70-80% of photo frame
  • Expression: Neutral, mouth closed, eyes open
  • Glasses: Not allowed since 2021 updated rules
  • Head Covering: Only for religious reasons with full face visible
  • Recency: Photo taken within last 3 months

Passport Photo PDF — Official Indian Passport Size

Indian passport ke liye standard photo size 3.5cm x 4.5cm hai aur PDF format mein submit karna hota hai Passport Seva Kendra ke online portal par. Yeh tool exactly Indian passport specifications follow karta hai.

Indian Passport Photo Rules

  • Size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm (exactly, not approximate)
  • Background: White ya light colored plain background
  • Expression: Neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open
  • Head Coverage: Face 70-80% of frame cover karna chahiye
  • No Glasses: 2023 se baad glasses allowed nahi hain passport photo mein

Passport Seva Online Application

passportindia.gov.in par fresh passport ya renewal ke liye apply karte waqt photo upload zaroori hai. Photo reject hone par appointment reschedule karna padta hai — isliye pehli baar mein sahi size ki photo upload karo.
