Official Page | Trust Verified

Our Verification Standards

PhotoSePDF.in is dedicated to providing candidates with accurate, up-to-date image dimensions and file sizes required for online registration forms. Incorrect photo uploads are the leading cause of application rejection in competitive exams, which is why our team follows a strict editorial verification protocol.

1. Source Notification Auditing

Before launching or updating any exam resizer preset (such as UP Police, SSC, BPSC, or UPSC), our editorial team led by Pradeep Meena reviews the official notification PDF published on the official recruitment portal. We verify the exact pixel dimensions (width & height), file format (usually JPEG), and file size limits (minimum & maximum KB constraints).

2. Regular Policy Updates

Government recruitment boards occasionally modify image requirements (for example, SSC's transition to live webcam captures or change in background rules). We audit and update our tools and instructions within 24 hours of any official policy changes.

3. Code of Transparency

Our tools are built using open Web APIs. We are transparent about our software logic, and candidates can check their output sizes and metadata on any computer before uploading to the official application form portals.

4. Fact-Checking Methodology

Every tool preset on PhotoSePDF.in undergoes a three-step fact-checking process before publication:

  • Primary Source Verification: We download and read the official recruitment notification PDF from the conducting body's website (e.g., ssc.nic.in, upsc.gov.in, upssonline.nic.in).
  • Cross-Reference Check: We verify the requirements against at least two independent sources.
  • Live Testing: Our team uploads a test image resized by our tool to the actual government portal to confirm acceptance before publishing the preset.

5. Correction Policy

If any user reports an inaccuracy, we follow this correction protocol:

  • The report is acknowledged within 24 hours via email.
  • Our editorial team led by Pradeep Meena verifies the claim against official sources within 48 hours.
  • If confirmed, the tool preset or article is updated immediately with a visible correction note.

Editorial Standards aur Fact-Checking Process

Humari editorial policy ka main focus accuracy, transparency, aur user trust par hai. Jo bhi content hum publish karte hain, especially exam guidelines aur document rules se related, uska ek strict fact-checking process hota hai. Hum official notifications (jaise SSC, UPSC, NTA websites) se data verify karte hain.

Content creation ke time, hum yeh ensure karte hain ki information simple aur easy-to-understand Hinglish aur English mein ho, taki har user ko samajh aaye. Agar koi rule change hota hai, toh humari team turant us update ko apni website par reflect karti hai.

Key Pillars of Our Policy

  • Accuracy: Official sources se cross-verification.
  • Clarity: Bina kisi jargons ke simple bhasha mein samjhana.
  • Timeliness: Latest updates aur modifications ko jald se jald lana.

Agar kabhi koi error highlight hota hai, toh hum use bina kisi delay ke correct karte hain aur transparent correction notice add karte hain. Aapka trust humare liye sabse important hai, aur us trust ko maintain karne ke liye hum hamesha highest editorial standards follow karte hain.

Content Verification Process

Dusra, hamare tools mein in specifications ko test kiya jaata hai — actual portal par upload karke. Teesra, reader feedback aur comments se content continuously update hota hai jab requirements change hoti hain.
